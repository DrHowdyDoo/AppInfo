# Privacy Policy for AppInfo

**Last updated: August 30, 2026**

## Overview

AppInfo ("the App") is a free and open-source Android application developed by **Avi Saxena / DrHowdyDoo**.

Your privacy is important to us. AppInfo is designed to work primarily on your device. The App does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share your personal data with the developer or third parties.

## Information Accessed by the App

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, AppInfo may access information available on your device, including:

* Information about installed applications and APK files
* Application package names and application metadata
* Application permissions, activities, services, providers, receivers, certificates, signatures, and related technical information
* Application usage information when you grant Usage Access permission for features such as sorting applications by usage
* Files and APKs stored on your device when you use features such as APK scanning, APK extraction, or font extraction
* Other application-related information required to provide AppInfo's functionality

This information is accessed solely to provide the features requested or used by you.

## No Data Collection

AppInfo does **not collect personal data or user data from your device and transmit it to the developer or any external server**.

All information accessed by AppInfo remains on your device and is processed locally.

AppInfo does not operate a server that receives or stores your application information, device information, usage information, files, APKs, or other data accessed by the App.

## No Data Sharing

AppInfo does not share user data with third parties.

AppInfo does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose user data to advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The App does not include advertising or analytics services that collect or transmit user data.

## Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

AppInfo does not maintain a remote database or server containing user data.

Any information processed by the App remains on your device.

Files intentionally created by the App, such as extracted APKs or fonts, are stored only on your device and can be deleted by you at any time using your device's file manager or the relevant functionality provided by the App.

AppInfo does not retain copies of your data outside your device.

## Permissions

AppInfo may request permissions or special access required to provide specific functionality.

For example:

* **Usage Access** may be used to display or sort applications based on usage-related information.
* **All Files Access** may be used for features such as scanning APK files, extracting APKs, or extracting fonts.

You can continue to use many features without granting optional permissions. Some features may not function if the required permission or access is denied.

Permissions are used only for the functionality associated with the permission and are not used to collect or transmit your data to external servers.

You can revoke permissions or special access at any time through your Android device settings.

## User-Initiated Sharing

AppInfo may allow you to explicitly share an APK, extracted file, Play Store link, or other content using Android's system sharing functionality.

Any such transfer occurs only when you deliberately initiate it and select the destination or receiving application.

AppInfo does not independently transmit these files or links.

Please note that once you choose to share content with another application or service, the privacy practices of that third party may apply.

## Third-Party Services

AppInfo does not use third-party services for advertising, analytics, tracking, or the collection of user data.

The App may use Android system components and platform services required for normal application functionality. Where you explicitly choose to interact with an external application or service, such as opening an app page on Google Play or sharing content through another app, that interaction is controlled by the privacy practices of the respective third party.

## Security

Because AppInfo does not transmit or store your data on external servers, your application and device information remains under your control on your device.

The App is designed to process information locally whenever required for its functionality.

## Children's Privacy

AppInfo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the App does not collect or transmit user data, no personal information is knowingly stored by the developer.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to AppInfo, applicable laws, or platform requirements.

Any updates will be published on the Privacy Policy page. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be updated accordingly.

## Open Source

AppInfo is an open-source project. The source code is publicly available for transparency and review.
